# KPI Dictionary

## Weighted pipeline
Open deal amount multiplied by stage probability. Grain: deal. Business use: expected value of current sales pipeline.

## Win rate
Won deals divided by all closed deals, where closed deals are Won or Lost. Grain: closed deal. Business use: sales effectiveness.

## Stage conversion
Number of deals in a stage divided by number of deals in the first stage in the synthetic funnel view. Business use: identifies funnel drop-off.

## Stalled opportunities
Open deals older than 45 days from created date. Business use: prompts sales follow-up and pipeline cleanup.

## Lead response time
Time between lead creation and first response. Business use: monitors inbound SLA and speed-to-lead.

## MRR
Monthly recurring revenue for active non-churned subscriptions plus active expansion MRR. Grain: subscription.

## ARR
MRR multiplied by 12. Business use: annualized recurring revenue view.

## Gross revenue retention
(Beginning MRR - churned MRR) / beginning MRR. Business use: retention before expansion.

## Net revenue retention
(Beginning MRR - churned MRR + expansion MRR) / beginning MRR. Business use: retention including expansion.

## Customer health score
Rule-based score from usage, onboarding, support, and renewal timing. Grain: subscription/company. Business use: flags accounts needing customer-success attention.
