# Change management and adoption plan

## Adoption risks
- Users may skip required CRM fields.
- Sales and Customer Success may define stages differently.
- Teams may distrust health scores if the logic is unclear.
- Managers may ask for KPIs without stable metric definitions.

## Adoption actions
1. Align lifecycle definitions before rollout.
2. Use mandatory fields only where they improve reporting quality.
3. Publish the KPI dictionary and review it with stakeholders.
4. Train sales users on lead, contact, company, and deal differences.
5. Train customer-success users on onboarding and renewal workflows.
6. Run weekly data-quality review during the first month.
7. Treat health score as a prioritization signal, not an automated decision.

## Success indicators
- Fewer duplicate accounts and contacts.
- More leads with first response timestamp.
- Fewer stalled deals without next task.
- Higher percentage of customers with complete onboarding tasks.
- At-risk accounts reviewed before renewal.
